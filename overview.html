Leadership

Thirty-five years of ISRO R&D, now leading SpaceLabs.

Co-Founder & CEO

Sheela DS

Ms. Sheela is a seasoned professional with over 35 years of R&D experience at the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). She is a distinguished expert in space vehicle guidance algorithm development, design, and mission planning and analysis. Throughout her career, she has played a pivotal role in shaping mission-critical technologies. At SpaceLabs, Ms. Sheela provides strategic leadership, actively guiding the organisation's vision, mission, and overall direction.

Our Mentors

Four former ISRO senior scientists, each with more than thirty-five years in launch vehicle control, navigation, aerodynamics and checkout systems.

Former Director, Inertial Systems Unit, ISRO

Dhekane MV

Distinguished Scientist with over 35 years of experience. Expert in launch vehicle control, guidance design, mission design and analysis, and onboard software. Formerly the Prof. Satish Dhawan Professor at IIST, where he led the student satellite programme. B.Tech in Electrical Engineering and M.Tech in Systems & Control, IIT Bombay.

FORMERLY ISRO · 35+ YEARS

Former Deputy Director, Inertial Systems Unit, ISRO

Abdul Shukkoor A

Over 35 years of expertise in navigation systems, algorithms and simulation. Played a key role in the Strapdown Inertial Navigation System (RESINS). Designed the navigation curriculum for DIAT, Pune.

FORMERLY ISRO · 35+ YEARS

Former Division Head, Aerodynamics, ISRO

Dr Dipankar Das

Distinguished aerodynamics expert with over 35 years of experience, 21 of them as a PhD holder, formerly heading Aerodynamics R&D and Aero Combustion Modelling at ISRO. Specialises in aero-thermal design of launch vehicles, venting analysis, aeroacoustics, and aero-structural dynamic response (flutter and buffet).

Sabu T
