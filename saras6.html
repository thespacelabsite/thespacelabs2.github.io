PRODUCT 02 — SARAS

SARAS

Simulator for Aerospace Applications

Test bed to simulate six degrees of freedom motion of an aerospace vehicle. SARAS simulates translational and rotational motion of the vehicle. The subsystems are mathematically modeled to realistically represent the vehicle and environment.

02 / 06 — CONFIGURATION & ANALYSIS

One test bed, configured per study.

The same vehicle model serves design, analysis and validation: run it as a design tool, batch it for statistical studies, or close the loop with software and hardware.

Scrolling this stage runs the simulation: the modelled vehicle sits nominal in its gimbal, a gust tilts it eighteen degrees, the navigation, guidance and control loop brings it back, and finally a hardware node at the cage corner lights to show the Ethernet link of a hardware-in-the-loop run.

03 / 06 — DISTURBANCE AND RECOVERY

HILS · ETHERNET

STEP 01 / 04

Model the vehicle

Mathematical models of dynamics and environment represent the vehicle realistically. SARAS computes all the forces and moments acting on it and simulates subsystems independently or in integrated mode.

STEP 02 / 04

Inject an off-nominal condition

A disturbance tilts the vehicle away from its commanded attitude. SARAS simulates the vehicle dynamics in off-nominal conditions, with real-time processing of multiple sensor data and display.

STEP 03 / 04

Close the loop

Navigation, Guidance and Control models — or the NGC/AOCS software running in the target processor — command the actuators and bring the attitude back to nominal.

STEP 04 / 04

Validate with hardware

In HILS the sensors and actuators are real hardware in the loop, communicating with the simulator over Ethernet. The same test bed becomes the NGC/AOCS system test bed.
