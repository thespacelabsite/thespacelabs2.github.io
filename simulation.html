SERVICE 05 — SIMULATION

Simulation

MILS · OILS · HILS

Validation and loop testing for mission-critical systems.

Simulation provides a cost-effective and time-efficient integrated testing environment for hardware and software, bridging the gap between concept and mission success.

What we validate The SARAS test bed →

Loop ring emblem: five blocks — NGC mathematical models, onboard processor, flight software, sensors and actuators — sit on a closed ring with signal pulses circulating. In MILS every block is simulated; in OILS the onboard processor and flight software are real; in HILS the processor, flight software, sensors and actuators are real hardware in the loop.

Three loops. One test bed.
